What we do

Three pieces, deliberately small.

Ad monetization, measurement and a shared SDK. All three are built for our own apps first; we do not sell them to other publishers.

01

Ad monetization

Banner placements across our own apps, plus the mediation and reporting behind them. Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network compete through mediation; we cap frequency per app and read revenue by placement, not as one blended number.

ScopeOur apps only. No third-party publishers.

02

Analytics

A small event pipeline for launches, sessions, retention checkpoints and ad impressions. We use a per-vendor identifier (IDFV), aggregate early, and avoid building profiles of individual users.

StoredAggregates and placement-level reports.

03

Lightweight SDK

Shared internal code for ad loading, consent and event logging. One job per module, no permissions it does not need, and privacy manifests that match what the code actually does.

StatusInternal only. Not licensed to other developers.

How we work

Boring choices, made on purpose.

We are early. That makes it easier to keep the product, the advertising and the data in proportion.

01

The app comes first.

An ad is a guest. If a placement makes the tool harder to use, it does not ship — regardless of what it would earn.

02

Collect less than we could.

No accounts, no contact uploads, no location. Identifiers stay per vendor, not per person, and reports are aggregated before we read them.

03

Own the boring parts.

Mediation, consent and event logging are our code. That is the only way we can explain every number and every data flow on this site.

04

Say what we don't do.

We are not an ad network, an agency or a data broker. We monetize our own apps and nothing else.

Boundaries

What we are — and what we are not.

What we are

  • An independent studio that owns and operates its own iOS apps.
  • Ad-supported: free tools, funded by a single banner placement where it fits.
  • Local-first: the content you create in our apps stays on your device.
  • Small: four people, one office, direct answers.

What we are not

  • An ad network. We do not sell other developers' inventory.
  • An agency. We do not run campaigns for clients.
  • A data business. We do not sell or resell personal information.
  • A “global leader”. We are early, and we say so.
