Photo cleanup

What does Best mean?

Best is a recommendation to help compare similar photos. You can change selections before confirming deletion. It does not lock a photo against your choices.

Does swiping left immediately delete a photo?

No. Left marks a photo for review; right keeps it. Review the marked list and confirm before removal. Any additional iOS confirmation also applies.

Why has free space not changed immediately?

Apple Photos may retain removed items in Recently Deleted. Device storage can take time to update. Review Recently Deleted in Photos when you are ready. Permanent removal can affect recovery, and iCloud Photos can synchronize confirmed changes to other devices.

Why are some photos or contacts missing?

Check the app's access in iOS Settings. Limited access permits only selected items. Cloud-backed originals may need to be retrieved by Apple's services before an operation can complete.

Videos, Live Photos and chat media

Does video compression keep the original?

Compression saves a separate copy. Review it before deciding whether to remove the original. Size and quality depend on the source and settings; some videos may not become smaller.

Does converting a Live Photo remove it?

Conversion saves a still copy. Removing the original Live Photo is a separate choice.

Can Cleaner Kit read my chats or clear another app's cache?

Chat media tools organize images and videos already saved in your Photos library. They do not sign in to messaging services, read private conversations or clear another app's private cache.

Contacts and private copies

What should I check before merging contacts?

Review matching names, phone numbers and email addresses. Shared details can belong to different people. The local vCard backup includes accessible fields only; protected or account-specific fields may be unavailable.

Are private album files backed up?

Private copies are encrypted on your device and excluded from backups. Export important copies before uninstalling or replacing your device. Originals in Photos are separate unless you remove them. We do not have a server recovery copy of your private album.

Do widgets always show live readings?

Widget refreshes are controlled by iOS. Values can reflect the most recent available update rather than a continuous live reading.

Purchases and subscriptions

How do I restore paid access?

Use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. An internet connection may be needed to verify access. Eligibility depends on purchase type and status.

Why do I not see the 3-day trial?

The trial is available only to eligible customers. If you have already used the introductory offer and are no longer eligible, the weekly option is shown without a free-trial promise. Apple determines eligibility for the relevant subscription group. The $0.77 weekly equivalent on an annual offer means $39.99 billed per year, not a weekly charge. Review the actual price and terms before confirming.

How do I cancel a subscription or trial?

Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions and manage it there. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription or trial. Follow the renewal or trial deadline shown by Apple.

Apple's cancellation instructions

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store refund requests. Follow Apple's refund process. Eligibility and approval depend on Apple's rules and applicable law.
