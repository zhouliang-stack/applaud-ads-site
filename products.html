Cleaner Kit: Phone Boost&Clean

Your cleanup toolkit.

Tools for the photos, videos, and contacts you choose to manage.

Roomy is becoming Cleaner Kit. The rebranded update, Cleaner Kit: Phone Boost&Clean, is under App Store review. The current U.S. listing still displays Roomy. Features vary by version.

Photos

Keep the shots you love.

Compare duplicate and similar photos, review screenshots, and choose what stays before confirming removal.

Videos

Smaller copies. More room.

Compress a selected video on your device. Check the new copy before separately deciding whether to remove the original.

Contacts

Bring the details together.

Review matching contact records and merge the details you choose. Check names and numbers before making a change.

Private album

A space for private copies.

Keep selected copies in a locally encrypted album. Export important files before removing the app or changing devices.

Swipe review

Keep. Review. Confirm.

Swipe right to keep a photo or left to mark it for review. A swipe alone does not permanently delete a photo.

Storage & widgets

See your space clearly.

View device storage information and supported widgets. Widget updates follow iOS refresh rules.

A few things to know

Video compression and Live Photo conversion save a separate copy. Review copies before removing originals. Chat media review works with items already saved in your Photos library; it does not read conversations or clear another app’s private cache. Cleaner Kit does not remove iOS system files or promise a particular speed or storage gain.

Analysis is free in the Cleaner Kit update. Cleanup and other Pro tools require a subscription or eligible trial. Availability depends on the installed version, device, permissions, and eligible access.
