01 · Layout checks

BannerGuard

A pre-flight check for banner ads.

Banner ads fail quietly. A few points of overlap with the home indicator, a close button too near a game control, a collision that only appears on a 4.7-inch screen — each one is easy to miss until review. BannerGuard turns those guesses into a short, repeatable checklist before a build goes out.

What it does

  • Measures banner sizes against safe-area insets on a range of device profiles.
  • Flags any tappable control that sits within 48 points of an ad frame.
  • Checks minimum spacing between a banner and persistent navigation.
  • Produces a plain pass/fail report you can attach to a build note.
  • Runs offline; project details never leave the device.

Advertising in this app

One banner at the bottom of the results screen. No interstitial, no ad while a check is running, and no “remove ads” subscription. The ad load is capped independently of how often the app is opened.

What it is not

A fill-rate or revenue tester. It checks layout, not demand; use your mediation dashboard for the commercial numbers.

02 · Revenue

AdLens

Ad revenue, without the spreadsheet fog.

Most indie developers read revenue in three places: an ad network, a spreadsheet and a note on their phone. AdLens collapses that into one timeline. Enter daily impressions and revenue by hand, or import a CSV export, and the app works out eCPM, estimated ARPDAU and week-over-week change.

What it does

What it is not

A real-time dashboard. AdLens does not connect to your Google or Meta accounts and fetches nothing automatically; it is a notebook that does the arithmetic.

03 · Cohorts

RetentionKit

Retention checkpoints, minus the warehouse.

RetentionKit is for the stage before a data team: you have daily counts and three questions. Are people coming back on day 1, day 7 and day 30 — and did the last release change that? Import an export, group installs into weekly cohorts, and compare two releases side by side.

Questions

Before you ask.

A few practical answers. Anything else goes to the inbox on the contact page.

Do the apps require an account or a sign-in?

No. None of the three apps has an account system, a cloud sync or a login wall. Everything you enter stays on the device unless you export it yourself.

Where is my data stored, and what happens if I delete the app?

Locally, inside the app’s own storage. We do not operate a sync service and cannot read your entries. Deleting the app removes that data; a device backup may keep a copy according to your iCloud or computer backup settings.

Why are the apps free?

Advertising pays for the work. BannerGuard and AdLens show a single banner on a summary or results screen; there are no subscriptions and no paid tiers. RetentionKit has no ads during its closed beta.

Do the apps collect my advertising identifier?

Only through the ad partners described in the Privacy Policy, and only within platform rules. On iOS, cross-app tracking requires your permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the apps still work and may show contextual ads.

How do I get a build link?

Email admin@applaud-ads.com with the product name. We send App Store links directly, plus TestFlight invitations for RetentionKit when a beta place is open.

Are the apps available on iPad or Mac?

They are iPhone-first. BannerGuard and AdLens run on iPad in compatibility mode; RetentionKit is iPhone-only during the beta. None of them is a Mac app.
