Cleaner Kit Privacy Policy

Last updated and effective date: November 5, 2026

This policy applies to Cleaner Kit: Phone Boost&Clean ("Cleaner Kit"), provided by APPLAUD ADS LIMITED, registered in Hong Kong. Contact admin@applaud-ads.com with privacy questions or requests.

At a glance

  • Photo, video and contact tools analyze the content you allow on your device. We do not send that content to AppsFlyer or our purchase-verification service.
  • We use AppsFlyer for attribution and analytics, and our server verifies App Store purchases. These services process the technical and transaction information described below.
  • You review and confirm deletions and contact merges. Device permissions and, where applicable, tracking authorization remain under your control.
  • Private album copies are encrypted locally and excluded from device backups. Export important copies before deleting the app or replacing your device.

1. Scope and responsibility

APPLAUD ADS LIMITED is responsible for personal information we control in connection with Cleaner Kit, our support and our websites. This policy distinguishes content processed by local tools from information sent to us or our service providers. Apple, your contact-account provider and independent websites you visit have their own policies.

2. Content used on your device

Tool Information used How it is used
Photo and video cleanup Permitted media, thumbnails and metadata such as size, date, dimensions and duration Local analysis, grouping, comparison, selection and changes you confirm
Video compression A selected video and quality settings Create a smaller copy on your device; compression does not remove the original

The app's local tools do not require a Cleaner Kit account. We do not operate a remote photo library or upload your photos, videos or contact book for external model training. Cleaner Kit does not connect to an email inbox or request messaging credentials. Chat media review does not clear another app's private cache.

3. Permissions, deletion and synchronization

Photos and Contacts permissions enable the corresponding tools. You can decline, grant the options offered by iOS, or change access in Settings. With limited access, the app can work only with the content made available to it. Revoking permission prevents future access; it does not undo changes already confirmed or remove copies already exported.

Swiping right keeps a photo; swiping left marks it for review. Removing marked photos requires your confirmation and any additional approval required by iOS. A Best label is a recommendation, not a restriction on your choices.

Apple Photos controls Recently Deleted and permanent removal. Free storage may not change immediately. If you use iCloud Photos or contact synchronization, confirmed changes can synchronize to other devices. Retrieving a cloud-backed original may involve Apple's services. Cleaner Kit does not operate those services or guarantee recovery of content it no longer has access to.

4. Private album and contact backups

Importing an item to the private album makes an encrypted local copy. The original stays in Photos unless separately removed. Private copies are excluded from device backups. Deleting the app, losing the device or losing access to local keys may make them unavailable. Export important files first. Decrypted exports are subject to the destination's protection.

If enabled, a PIN or supported device biometrics protect private-album access. iOS performs biometric verification; we do not receive your facial template or fingerprint.

Before a confirmed contact merge, the app makes a local vCard backup of accessible fields. Protected or account-specific fields may be unavailable. A backup you share goes to the destination you select. A widget may expose its displayed information to anyone who can see your screen.

5. Attribution and analytics: AppsFlyer

We use AppsFlyer to understand acquisition sources, measure app use and purchases, evaluate campaigns and detect attribution fraud. Depending on the integration and your choices, information processed can include device and operating-system details, language, IP address, timestamps, installation and launch events, in-app activity such as purchase or subscription events, campaign information, and installation or device identifiers such as AppsFlyer ID and IDFV. An IP address can indicate a general region; this does not require access to your device's GPS location.

The advertising identifier, IDFA, is accessed only when permitted by iOS and the authorization you give. Where our setup involves tracking across other companies' apps or websites for advertising measurement, we request the authorization required by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before that tracking occurs. Refusing tracking does not prevent use of the local cleanup tools. Some aggregate measurement or non-tracking analytics can continue where permitted by law and platform rules.

We do not send your photo or video content, contact names or contact-book contents to AppsFlyer. Technical identifiers can still be personal information even when they do not include your name.

6. Purchases and server verification

Apple processes App Store payments and provides purchase information so eligible access can be enabled and restored. We do not receive your full payment-card number from Apple.

Our verification service processes the app receipt or purchase-verification information, product and transaction identifiers, the original transaction identifier where applicable, app identifier, purchase or subscription status and related dates. Ordinary server requests also involve technical information such as IP address and request time. We use this information to verify purchases, restore access, prevent fraud, maintain subscription status, handle support and meet applicable obligations. Purchase or subscription events may also be associated with attribution identifiers for measurement and deduplication.

Your Photos library and contact book are not required for purchase verification. Apple's purchase records, billing and refund handling are governed by Apple's terms and privacy practices.

7. Support and websites

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any name, attachments, transaction references or device details you choose to include. We use them to answer the request, investigate the issue, respond to privacy inquiries and keep necessary records. Send only the information needed; your photo library and contacts are not automatically attached.

Opening an online page creates a browser or web-view request. The host receives information such as IP address, requested page, time and browser details, and may retain access or security logs. These legal pages do not embed advertising, analytics scripts or externally hosted fonts. Independent sites you open have their own practices.

9. Recipients and international processing

Recipients include AppsFlyer for the measurement described above, Apple for its App Store and system services, and infrastructure, hosting and email providers to the extent needed to deliver our services. We restrict information handled on our behalf to the relevant purposes. Configured advertising-measurement integrations may involve campaign providers where permitted by your choices, applicable law and platform rules.

We may disclose information we actually hold when legally required, or where necessary and permitted to address security, fraud, rights or legal claims. This does not give us access to local content we do not hold.

Our company and service providers may process information outside your country, including Hong Kong and countries in which the providers operate. Where required, we use applicable contractual or other lawful safeguards for international transfers. Contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data.

10. Retention and deletion

Local preferences, working data, encrypted copies and backups follow app and iOS storage controls until replaced or removed. Delete App removes local app data under iOS rules; offloading is different. Uninstalling does not reverse confirmed Photos or Contacts changes, delete exports or cancel a subscription. Synchronized copies and system backups follow their service settings.

We keep purchase-verification and necessary transaction records for the time needed to maintain access, restore purchases, prevent fraud, resolve disputes and comply with applicable financial or legal obligations. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve the request, provide follow-up and meet applicable obligations. Website and server logs follow operational and security needs and applicable law.

Attribution and analytics retention follows the applicable AppsFlyer settings, integration, partner requirements and law. Reporting access windows are not necessarily the same as retention periods. We limit identifiable records to the purposes described here; properly aggregated or de-identified statistics may be retained where permitted. Provider backups can expire on a separate cycle.

Contact us to request deletion of information we hold. We will explain any lawful retention exception. Deleting the app does not itself delete server-side transaction or attribution records.

11. Your choices

  • Change Photos or Contacts access in iOS Settings.
  • Change an app's tracking authorization in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking where available. This controls tracking authorization, not every form of permitted analytics.
  • Review and confirm content changes, export important private copies, and remove widgets you do not want displayed.
  • Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Account billing records remain subject to Apple's controls.
  • Email admin@applaud-ads.com about privacy rights, applicable analytics or advertising-measurement choices, or information we hold.

12. Privacy rights

Depending on the applicable law and processing, rights may include information about processing, access, a copy, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection and withdrawal of consent. You may complain to a competent privacy regulator. Hong Kong rights include data access and correction under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance. Where EEA, UK or Swiss law applies, its relevant rights and supervisory-authority routes apply.

Applicable U.S. state law may also provide rights concerning sale, sharing or targeted advertising, limits on certain sensitive-information uses, appeals of denied requests and protection from unlawful discrimination. We do not sell your photo library or contact-book contents. Advertising-measurement disclosures can be treated as sharing or targeted advertising under some laws; applicable opt-out rights and consent requirements are respected. We do not make solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you through media recommendations.

Send requests to admin@applaud-ads.com. An authorized representative may act where allowed. We may ask for proportionate information to verify identity or locate records, and respond within applicable deadlines. We explain permitted refusals, extensions and applicable appeal routes. We cannot remotely access device-only content; use the local app and system controls for that content.

13. Security

We use iOS permissions, local storage protections and encryption for private copies. We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to information handled by us or on our behalf, including access restrictions and secure transmission where appropriate. No device, service or transmission is guaranteed completely secure. We provide incident notices where required by law. Keep your device protected and export important copies before removing local storage.

14. Young users

Cleaner Kit is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to solicit children's personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided information to us may contact us for review and deletion where appropriate. Applicable parental-authorization and child-privacy requirements apply to the relevant activity. This policy does not replace the App Store age rating.

15. Policy changes and contact

We update this policy and its date when practices change. Material changes receive appropriate notice and any required consent. The current policy is available through the app and its App Store listing.
