Roomy Privacy Policy

Effective date: Upon publication

APPLAUD ADS LIMITED, a company registered in Hong Kong ("we", "us" or "our"), provides Roomy (the "App"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use Roomy, contact us, or open this policy online. It describes the App's current practices. A feature mentioned below applies only when it is available in your version and you choose to use it.

At a glance

  • Roomy processes the photos, videos and other device information used by its features on your device. The App does not upload this content, its analysis results or your activity to us or to third parties.
  • The App has no account registration, advertising, analytics, attribution tools or third-party SDKs. We do not receive automatic usage or crash reports from code within the App.
  • You control permissions, review your choices and confirm deletion. Swiping left marks an item for possible deletion; it does not immediately delete it. Swiping right keeps it.
  • If you choose to email us, we receive your email and anything you include. Opening a website also involves a separate request to the web host. These voluntary interactions are explained separately below.

1. Who is responsible and how to contact us

APPLAUD ADS LIMITED is responsible for Roomy and for personal information we receive through our support and privacy contact channel. Where applicable law uses the terms "data user" or "controller", we act in that role for that information.

For questions, privacy requests or concerns, email admin@applaud-ads.com. Please identify Roomy in your message. You do not need to send your photo library or contact list to ask a question.

2. Information used on your device

Photos and videos. With the permission you grant through iOS, Roomy accesses the media available to it in your Photos library. This can include media previews and information needed to display, group or review items, such as media type, dimensions, creation date and video duration. Roomy uses that access to help you review similar photos, videos, similar videos, screenshots, similar screenshots, potentially blurry photos and other photos, and to remove items you decide not to keep.

Grouping and blur suggestions are aids to your review. Similar items are not necessarily identical, and intentional blur may appear among suggestions. You decide what to keep. The App does not send the media, its metadata, suggestions, selections or derived analysis results off your device.

Media saved from chat apps. This feature concerns media already saved to your Photos library. It does not give Roomy access to your private conversations, chat account or another app's internal storage. Not every saved item can necessarily be recognized.

Contacts, where available. If you choose a contact management feature and grant Contacts permission, Roomy uses the contacts made available by iOS, including the relevant names and contact details, only on your device to provide the feature you select. It does not upload your address book, use it to contact people, or build a contact database for us.

Widgets, where available. Roomy's widgets use locally available information and settings to display their supported content or open App features. Any information shared between Roomy and its widget stays in their on-device storage. Widgets do not report information to our servers.

Local settings and working information. The App may retain preferences and working information needed for its features, such as review selections, thumbnails or scan results, in its local storage. These are used to operate the App, not to monitor your behavior remotely.

Your library may contain sensitive or private material. On-device access does not mean that we can view it. Roomy does not transmit that material to us, to an advertising service or to an external AI service, and we do not use it to train an external model.

3. Permissions and your choices

Roomy uses the access you authorize through Apple's permission controls. Where iOS offers limited Photos or Contacts access, the App can access only the items iOS makes available under that choice. Reviews and results are limited to that accessible information; they should not be interpreted as a complete review of items the App cannot access.

You can deny permission, change the selected items or revoke access in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security or Roomy's settings, depending on your iOS version. Features that need the denied information may be unavailable or show fewer items. Granting access to Photos or Contacts does not authorize advertising or tracking.

Revoking access stops future access through that permission. It does not reverse changes you previously confirmed in your Photos library or Contacts, and it does not necessarily erase existing local preferences or working files. Deleting the App removes its associated local app data as managed by iOS; offloading an app can retain its documents and data.

4. Review, deletion and Apple's services

In the swipe review flow, left means mark for deletion, right means keep, and the final review lets you confirm what to delete. Roomy submits the deletion you confirm to the relevant iOS system service; you must also approve any confirmation required by iOS. A suggestion or swipe alone is not permission for automatic deletion.

Deletion from Photos is governed by Apple's library and Recently Deleted behavior. Recovery availability and the time before permanent removal depend on Apple's services and your circumstances. Roomy does not keep a server backup and cannot restore a file it no longer has access to.

If you have enabled iCloud Photos, contact synchronization, device backups or similar Apple services, Apple may synchronize your library and changes across your devices or keep backups according to your settings. For example, a confirmed deletion from an iCloud Photos library can also remove the item from other devices using that library. Accessing a cloud-backed library may involve Apple's own retrieval of media. These system services are separate from Roomy's on-device analysis; Roomy does not operate a cloud library or send your content to an App backend. Apple's services are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Information Roomy does not send

The App does not send us photos, videos, contacts, widget content, scan results, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage events or diagnostic reports. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, sell personal information, or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. It does not connect to an email inbox or collect chat credentials.

Apple may separately process App Store, operating-system or diagnostic information under its own settings and policies. The absence of a Roomy analytics SDK does not change Apple's independent services.

6. If you contact us

If you voluntarily send an email to admin@applaud-ads.com, we receive your email address, message and any name, attachments or technical details you choose to provide. We use these only to respond, investigate the issue you raise, handle privacy requests and keep necessary records of the correspondence. You can use Roomy's local features without emailing us.

Please avoid sending passwords, identity documents, private media or other sensitive information unless it is necessary for a specific request and an appropriate method has been agreed. We do not automatically attach your photo library, contact list or App activity to an email.

Email delivery and storage involve the email providers used by you and us. We limit access to correspondence to people and providers who need it to handle the inquiry, protect it appropriately and do not use your correspondence for advertising. Any provider we engage to process information on our behalf must use it only for the agreed service and provide the same or an equivalent level of protection as this policy and applicable requirements. We may disclose information we actually hold when legally required, or when necessary and permitted by law to protect rights or address security issues. This does not give us access to your on-device library.

7. The online policy page and external links

Roomy's local processing does not require uploading content to a policy website. If you open this policy or another online page, your browser or system web view makes an ordinary network request. The destination's web host receives technical request information, such as the IP address, requested page, time and browser information, and may retain access or security logs to deliver and protect the website. That website traffic is separate from the App's local media processing and does not include your photo library or contacts.

This policy page does not include advertising, analytics scripts or externally hosted fonts. Your browser may expose additional information through its own settings. Other websites and services you choose to open have their own privacy practices. Contact us if you need details about the hosting of this policy page or wish to make a request about website information.

8. Purposes and legal grounds

Roomy's on-device access is limited to providing the feature you request and is controlled by your system permissions. We do not repurpose your library for marketing, remote profiling or external model training.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for personal information we receive outside that local processing, we use the basis appropriate to the activity: responding to a service request or taking steps you request; our legitimate interests in providing support and keeping our communications and website secure, balanced against your rights; compliance with a legal obligation; or consent when the law requires it. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. A system permission is not a blanket consent to unrelated uses.

9. Retention and deletion

On-device information: Your library remains under the control of Photos, Contacts and your device settings. Local App preferences and working information remain only as needed by the corresponding feature, until replaced, cleared or removed with the App under iOS storage rules. Removing Roomy does not by itself delete your original Photos library or Contacts. Previously confirmed library changes are not undone by uninstalling it. Backups and synchronized copies are subject to the settings and deletion controls of the relevant system service.

Correspondence: We retain support and privacy messages only for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain a necessary record, comply with applicable retention obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. The criteria include whether the issue remains open and whether a legal obligation requires retention. You can request deletion at admin@applaud-ads.com; if retention is required, we will explain the applicable limitation. Routine email backups may take additional time to expire under the provider's deletion cycle.

Website information: Any web-host logs are separate from App data and are subject to the host's operational retention settings and applicable law. You can contact us about the relevant records. We have no server copy of your Roomy library to search, export or delete.

10. Providers, sharing and international handling

There are no third-party SDKs or App-data recipients for Roomy's local features. We do not sell or rent the content the App accesses. An email or hosting provider can process the limited correspondence or website request information needed for its service. Such providers may operate in countries outside yours. Where applicable law requires transfer safeguards, we will apply the safeguards required for the relevant service and can provide information on request. This statement does not mean your on-device media is transferred abroad by Roomy.

11. Security

Roomy's local features use iOS permission controls and app storage protections. Keeping media processing on your device avoids a Roomy server copy of that media. Protect your device with its available security settings and keep iOS updated.

For information you choose to send to us, we use reasonable safeguards and limit access to those who need it. No device, email service or website can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If an incident requires notice under applicable law, we will provide that notice.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and which laws apply to the relevant processing, you may have rights to obtain information about processing, access or receive a copy of personal information, correct it, request deletion, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or receive portable data where the applicable conditions are met. You may also have a right to complain to a competent privacy regulator. In Hong Kong, applicable rights include requesting access to and correction of personal data under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance. You may contact the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data, Hong Kong about a concern. Where EEA, UK or Swiss data protection law applies, the relevant rights and supervisory authority complaint routes apply under that law.

Where applicable US state privacy law provides such rights, you may also have rights to opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising, request a review or appeal of a denied request, and receive equal treatment when exercising a privacy right. Roomy does not sell information or use it for targeted advertising. It does not make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you; media suggestions remain subject to your review.

Send requests to admin@applaud-ads.com and describe the information and action concerned. Where permitted by law, an authorized representative may contact us on your behalf. We may request proportionate information to confirm identity or authority, without asking for unrelated library content. We respond within the period required by applicable law and explain any permitted refusal, extension or appeal route. Rights are subject to their legal conditions and exceptions. We do not collect additional personal information solely to create a record we do not otherwise hold.

For content held only on your device, use the App, Photos, Contacts and Settings controls described above; we cannot remotely access that content to carry out a request.

13. Children and young users

Roomy does not collect personal information from children through its local features. It is a general-purpose device utility, not a service designed to solicit children's information. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information by email can contact admin@applaud-ads.com so we can assess and delete it where appropriate. If local law requires parental authorization for a particular activity, that requirement applies. This policy does not set a universal age of consent or replace the App Store age rating.

We will update this policy and its date when our practices change. If a change materially affects how personal information is handled, we will give appropriate notice before it takes effect and obtain any consent required by law or Apple's rules. We will also update relevant App Store privacy disclosures. A later feature or SDK is not authorized by this policy merely because it might be added in the future.
