4. Review before deletion or merging

Suggestions identify items for your review. Best is a recommendation, not an instruction that prevents another choice. Swiping right keeps a photo; left marks it for review. Removal requires confirmation and any additional system approval.

Photos controls Recently Deleted, permanent removal and recovery. Device free space may not update immediately. iCloud Photos or contact-account synchronization can apply changes to other devices. Keep appropriate backups. The app is not a recovery service and cannot restore inaccessible content.

Contact records can share numbers or email addresses while belonging to different people. Review matching records and fields before merging. A local vCard backup contains accessible fields only; protected or account-specific fields may not be available.

5. Compression, conversion and private copies

Video compression creates a new copy. Size and quality depend on the source and settings; no fixed reduction or lossless result is guaranteed. Review the saved copy before separately removing an original. Live Photo conversion saves a still copy, and removal of the original is a separate choice.

Private album files are encrypted local copies, excluded from device backups. Originals remain in Photos unless separately removed. Export important files before deleting the app, changing devices or losing access to local storage or keys. Decrypted exports are protected by their destination. iOS verifies device biometrics; we do not receive your biometric template.
