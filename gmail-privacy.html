Part A Google and Gmail data

This part covers the planned Gmail permission and account connection, the information used for message organization, storage and security, permitted transfers, retention, user-confirmed mailbox changes, and disconnection and deletion controls.

1. Access you choose

You will be able to choose whether to connect a Gmail account through Google’s authorization flow. Cleaner Kit will not ask for or receive your Google password. Declining Gmail access will not grant access to your mailbox or prevent use of unrelated tools, subject to their normal permissions and purchase requirements.

The planned Gmail permission is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify. Google describes this permission broadly, including reading, composing, and sending messages. Cleaner Kit’s planned use is narrower: identify and display messages for your review, and move only messages you select and confirm to Gmail Trash. The planned feature will not compose or send messages, automatically unsubscribe you, run server-side scheduled cleanup, or permanently delete messages. It will not request permission to bypass Trash and permanently delete messages.

2. Gmail information used and why

After your authorization, the feature will use your connected account’s email address and Gmail message or thread identifiers, sender and recipient headers, subjects, dates, size estimates, labels or categories, and read/unread status. These fields will support account identification, grouping and filtering, review of candidate messages, and applying the cleanup you confirm.

The planned scanning and grouping flow will use message metadata rather than download message bodies or attachment contents. Email information will be used only for the Gmail organization tools you choose. Suggestions will not replace your review or authorize a cleanup on their own.

3. Direct connection and storage

The planned design connects your device directly to Google’s OAuth services and Gmail API over encrypted connections. Mailbox scanning and grouping will take place on your device. Gmail content, metadata, connected-account identifiers, and access or refresh tokens will not be uploaded to our servers, our purchase-verification service, AppsFlyer, advertising services, or third-party AI services.

Authorization tokens will be held in protected, device-only credential storage for the connection you choose to keep. Mailbox working data will be kept in app memory for the active Gmail session and cleared when you leave that session or disconnect. The planned feature will not maintain a persistent mailbox database or cloud backup of Gmail working data. Your original mailbox remains with Google.

4. Changes and Gmail Trash

You will review the selected messages and confirm before Cleaner Kit sends a move-to-Trash request to Google. The planned feature will not empty Trash or request permanent deletion. Changes apply to your Google account and can appear in Gmail and on other devices.

Gmail normally retains messages in Trash for up to 30 days before automatically deleting them. You may restore messages through Gmail before permanent deletion, subject to Google’s rules and any applicable account policies. If you or Google permanently removes a message, Cleaner Kit cannot promise recovery. Review Google’s message deletion guidance.

Moving messages to Trash does not immediately reduce your Google storage usage. Gmail organization also does not directly free iPhone storage. Counts and size estimates are not a promise of immediately recovered storage.

5. Sharing, analytics, and Limited Use

Gmail data will be exchanged only between your device and Google as needed to provide the authorized Gmail feature. We will not sell Gmail data, use it for advertising or profiling, combine it with advertising identifiers, or share it with AppsFlyer, data brokers, or external AI providers. Gmail data will not be used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Our staff will not have routine access to your mailbox through this device-only design.

Existing attribution, purchase-verification, support, and website services are described in Part B below and in the current App Store Privacy Policy. Their existence does not permit Gmail data or authorization tokens to be included in analytics events, diagnostic payloads, purchase-verification requests, or server logs.

Cleaner Kit’s use and any transfer of Google API information will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Gmail data will be used only to provide the user-facing Gmail tools described here.

6. Disconnect, revoke access, and delete working data

You will be able to disconnect the Google account in the Gmail feature. Disconnecting will stop future app access and remove locally stored connection tokens and Gmail working data. You can also revoke Cleaner Kit’s access in your Google Account connections. Revoking access does not undo cleanup requests already completed or delete the original mailbox at Google.

Removing the app will remove its local working data under iOS rules; device-only credentials will not be configured to migrate through backups or synchronization. Because uninstalling an app is not the same as revoking a Google authorization, use the disconnect control and Google Account settings before removal when you want to end that authorization.

7. Support, security, and your rights

Protect your device and review account permissions. No device or service can be guaranteed completely secure. If you contact support, do not send Google passwords, authorization tokens, or unrelated message content. Information you voluntarily put in a support email is handled under the current Privacy Policy and is separate from automated Gmail access.

For privacy rights, security issues, or questions about data practices, contact admin@applaud-ads.com. Applicable rights and international processing of support or website requests are explained in Part B below and the current App Store Privacy Policy. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase mailbox working data that exists only on your device; use the app and Google controls for that data.

8. Availability and changes

Before the Gmail feature is released, its app behavior, permission request, and notices must match these stated practices. If its design changes, this notice and any required user notice or authorization will be updated before the changed processing starts. Current app purchase terms and non-Gmail data practices are not replaced by this future-feature notice.

Google API data will not be used to create or generate images or other synthetic media. The Gmail feature does not provide generative image tools, and Gmail data will not be used to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

Part B Complete app data practices

The disclosures in this part carry forward the current app policy. They describe the non-Gmail tools, AppsFlyer, purchase verification, support, website requests, recipients, legal bases, retention and privacy rights. The future Gmail restrictions in Part A remain separate from these general practices.

At a glance

  • Photo, video and contact tools analyze the content you allow on your device. We do not send that content to AppsFlyer or our purchase-verification service.
  • We use AppsFlyer for attribution and analytics, and our server verifies App Store purchases. These services process the technical and transaction information described below.
  • You review and confirm deletions and contact merges. Device permissions and, where applicable, tracking authorization remain under your control.
  • Private album copies are encrypted locally and excluded from device backups. Export important copies before deleting the app or replacing your device.

This policy distinguishes content processed by local tools from information sent to us or our service providers. Apple, your contact-account provider and independent websites you visit have their own policies.

3. Permissions, deletion and synchronization

Photos and Contacts permissions enable the corresponding tools. You can decline, grant the options offered by iOS, or change access in Settings. With limited access, the app can work only with the content made available to it. Revoking permission prevents future access; it does not undo changes already confirmed or remove copies already exported.

Swiping right keeps a photo; swiping left marks it for review. Removing marked photos requires your confirmation and any additional approval required by iOS. A Best label is a recommendation, not a restriction on your choices.

Apple Photos controls Recently Deleted and permanent removal. Free storage may not change immediately. If you use iCloud Photos or contact synchronization, confirmed changes can synchronize to other devices. Retrieving a cloud-backed original may involve Apple's services. Cleaner Kit does not operate those services or guarantee recovery of content it no longer has access to.

4. Private album and contact backups

Importing an item to the private album makes an encrypted local copy. The original stays in Photos unless separately removed. Private copies are excluded from device backups. Deleting the app, losing the device or losing access to local keys may make them unavailable. Export important files first. Decrypted exports are subject to the destination's protection.

If enabled, a PIN or supported device biometrics protect private-album access. iOS performs biometric verification; we do not receive your facial template or fingerprint.

Before a confirmed contact merge, the app makes a local vCard backup of accessible fields. Protected or account-specific fields may be unavailable. A backup you share goes to the destination you select. A widget may expose its displayed information to anyone who can see your screen.

5. Attribution and analytics: AppsFlyer

We use AppsFlyer to understand acquisition sources, measure app use and purchases, evaluate campaigns and detect attribution fraud. Depending on the integration and your choices, information processed can include device and operating-system details, language, IP address, timestamps, installation and launch events, in-app activity such as purchase or subscription events, campaign information, and installation or device identifiers such as AppsFlyer ID and IDFV. An IP address can indicate a general region; this does not require access to your device's GPS location.

The advertising identifier, IDFA, is accessed only when permitted by iOS and the authorization you give. Where our setup involves tracking across other companies' apps or websites for advertising measurement, we request the authorization required by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before that tracking occurs. Refusing tracking does not prevent use of the local cleanup tools. Some aggregate measurement or non-tracking analytics can continue where permitted by law and platform rules.

We do not send your photo or video content, contact names or contact-book contents to AppsFlyer. Technical identifiers can still be personal information even when they do not include your name.

6. Purchases and server verification

Apple processes App Store payments and provides purchase information so eligible access can be enabled and restored. We do not receive your full payment-card number from Apple.

Our verification service processes the app receipt or purchase-verification information, product and transaction identifiers, the original transaction identifier where applicable, app identifier, purchase or subscription status and related dates. Ordinary server requests also involve technical information such as IP address and request time. We use this information to verify purchases, restore access, prevent fraud, maintain subscription status, handle support and meet applicable obligations. Purchase or subscription events may also be associated with attribution identifiers for measurement and deduplication.

Your Photos library and contact book are not required for purchase verification. Apple's purchase records, billing and refund handling are governed by Apple's terms and privacy practices.

7. Support and websites

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any name, attachments, transaction references or device details you choose to include. We use them to answer the request, investigate the issue, respond to privacy inquiries and keep necessary records. Send only the information needed; your photo library and contacts are not automatically attached.

Opening an online page creates a browser or web-view request. The host receives information such as IP address, requested page, time and browser details, and may retain access or security logs. These legal pages do not embed advertising, analytics scripts or externally hosted fonts. Independent sites you open have their own practices.

10. Retention and deletion

Local preferences, working data, encrypted copies and backups follow app and iOS storage controls until replaced or removed. Delete App removes local app data under iOS rules; offloading is different. Uninstalling does not reverse confirmed Photos or Contacts changes, delete exports or cancel a subscription. Synchronized copies and system backups follow their service settings.

We keep purchase-verification and necessary transaction records for the time needed to maintain access, restore purchases, prevent fraud, resolve disputes and comply with applicable financial or legal obligations. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve the request, provide follow-up and meet applicable obligations. Website and server logs follow operational and security needs and applicable law.

Attribution and analytics retention follows the applicable AppsFlyer settings, integration, partner requirements and law. Reporting access windows are not necessarily the same as retention periods. We limit identifiable records to the purposes described here; properly aggregated or de-identified statistics may be retained where permitted. Provider backups can expire on a separate cycle.

Contact us to request deletion of information we hold. We will explain any lawful retention exception. Deleting the app does not itself delete server-side transaction or attribution records.
